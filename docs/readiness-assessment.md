On 6 November 2026, Wrangler reported no pending migrations for the remote `rnig-investments` D1 database. This confirms migration version alignment at that time; it does not validate the correctness or completeness of the database contents.

The credit-booking fulfilment tests check partial fulfilments, retry behavior, over-fulfilment prevention and exclusion of voided fulfilments. The report repository tests also exercise exact totals above JavaScript's safe-integer range; both targeted test groups passed on 6 November 2026.

The date-input source scan on 6 November 2026 found no direct date inputs outside the shared `DatePickerInput` implementation. Its date parsing and formatting tests passed in both Africa/Kampala and America/Los_Angeles time zones. This scan does not replace interaction testing of every form.

On 6 November 2026, the deployed board-pack form's start-date calendar was used to select 1 November 2026, and a separate check selected 6 November 2026 in its end-date calendar. The form displayed each selected value; the end-date selection also updated the share-sale request message. In a further check, selecting 6 November as the start date set the end-date minimum to that date: 5 November was disabled in the calendar and 6 November remained selectable. No report was submitted during these checks.

`docs/questions-for-gm.md` contains the questions and source records needed to complete the unresolved financial work. No unconfirmed formula, valuation, ownership transfer or settlement should be treated as an approved rule.

The application source, migrations and tests have local commits on `main`. The commits have not been pushed to the public GitHub repository. A separate source snapshot was saved in Documents on 7 November 2026. The administrator document register now uses a private Cloudflare R2 bucket; see [supporting documents](supporting-documents.md) for its limits.

On 6 November 2026, an isolated local browser test saved a synthetic credit booking while a register lookup was deliberately unavailable. The register hid its forms and confirmed that the booking had been recorded; after the lookup was restored and the page reloaded, the booking appeared. This verifies the booking loader-failure path, not every possible network or routing failure. Type checking and the production build passed, and Worker version `75e91a65-75ac-4f1e-a4bc-734e3b3ce0e0` was deployed.

A local browser inspection on 6 November 2026 displayed a synthetic repayment acknowledgment with the member, amount, payment date and linked journal. The browser automation timed out when invoking the print action, so print-preview layout and actual printing remain unverified.

On 7 November 2026, a synthetic document replacement was recorded locally with its reason and link to the original. A local HTTP integration test checked administrator upload/download, member and unauthenticated denial, and cross-origin rejection. An isolated board-pack print harness verified selected-draft content preparation and cleanup; browser print preview still timed out, so printed rendering remains unverified. The document and print changes were deployed as Worker version `bb228b9d-4771-4e38-80cc-026289b7e7a3`.

On 7 November 2026, the source archive and Git bundle were restored in disposable locations. Synthetic local D1 and R2 drills checked SQL export/import and PDF bytes and metadata respectively; they did not test live-data or off-account restoration. See [backup and recovery](backup-and-recovery.md).

On 6 November 2026, an isolated local browser test recorded a synthetic group-buyer trade and matching seller sale event, then reviewed and linked them through trade reconciliation. The history showed the group as buyer with no buyer share event. This verifies recording and reconciliation only; it does not verify settlement, transfer to the group or the unresolved buyback pricing and approval rules.

On 6 November 2026, four integration checks passed against a disposable local Worker and D1 copy: email/password sign-in and sign-out with an unverified test account, disabled public sign-up, one-use member invitation and linked portal access, cross-origin request rejection, and safe server-rendered login form behavior. These checks do not validate real email delivery.

Password recovery uses Resend at the user's request. The Resend dashboard shows fidexa.org as verified, and the Worker has an encrypted RESEND_API_KEY secret. Recovery remains disabled; configuring the sender and enable flag and verifying controlled-mailbox delivery are still outstanding. No Cloudflare subscription upgrade or DNS changes were made.

On 6 November 2026, a local browser test previewed and saved a board-pack draft for a synthetic reporting period with a selected share-sale request. It included lending and a balanced trial balance and identified omitted optional sections. A subsequent backdated synthetic investment transaction did not appear when the saved draft was reopened, confirming that this tested draft retained its captured contents. Account mappings and source data remain unapproved.

A local browser test on 6 November 2026 granted a synthetic member account access to a synthetic saved board draft and confirmed that the member could view its expandable sections. The browser automation stalled at the revocation confirmation dialog, so revocation was not manually verified in that run; automated access tests cover revocation and inactive or replaced links. No production access was changed.
