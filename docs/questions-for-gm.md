# Questions for the general manager

## Loan interest and repayments

1. What is the current interest formula for Kanungu member loans: reducing balance, simple interest, or compounding? How do the stated 10% per year and 5% per six months relate?
2. What day-count convention, rounding, accrual frequency and due-date rules should be used? When do rate changes take effect on existing loans?
3. How are partial and late repayments allocated between interest, principal and any other charges? Please provide worked examples and the loan spreadsheet used today.
4. How is borrowing capacity calculated across multiple loans, and which share valuation and date apply? How is the stated 2% per month on excess borrowing approved and calculated?

## Shares and recovery

5. How is the conservative share price calculated and approved, and which price applies to valuation, collateral, exits and group buybacks?
6. What are the default triggers, approvals, share-sale sequence and allocation of proceeds? What happens to unsold shares or a remaining debt?
7. What are the rules for share-sale notices, anonymous offers, bids, first-come allocation, partial fills, settlement and board approval?

## Accounts and reporting

8. What is the exact legal and reporting relationship between RNIG and Melrah Investment Limited? Which records and financial statements belong to each?
9. Which roles can see member identities, individual holdings, loans and group financial records, and who can change or approve each record?
10. What is the authoritative current IPS, asset classification, allocation basis, budget and reporting calendar? Which valuation basis should each report use?
11. Please provide and validate the current share register, loan and credit-booking spreadsheets, IPS tracker, opening balances, accounting policies and reconciled financial statements. How should the unbalanced figures in the supplied statements be resolved?
