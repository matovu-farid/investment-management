# Financial decision register

These decisions require confirmation by the general manager and, where appropriate, the board. The calls and their automated transcripts are a starting point, not an approved financial specification. Until a decision is recorded and validated against source documents, the system must not imply that an inferred calculation or workflow is authoritative.

## Evidence to obtain

Request the current shareholding register, IPS and tracker, latest financial statements and supporting accounting policies, loan spreadsheets and credit-booking records, relevant meeting minutes and IPO material. Have the GM validate imported records and proposed accounting and calculation examples before enabling automated financial postings or decisions.

## Evidence from documents supplied in November 2026

Page numbers below are PDF page numbers. These are historical source documents and reports, not by themselves authorization to automate a current rule or import balances. The name appearing in the documents is **Melrah Investments Limited**; verify its registered name and relationship with RNIG.

- **Rates vary by period and product.** The 2020 prospectus describes member credit at 3% per 60 days or 18% annually (prospectus p. 8), while the calls describe 10% annually and disagree on the calculation method. The 2026 statements describe 17% or 18% monthly reducing-balance terms for **bank borrowing by the company**, not member loans (statements p. 34; board pack p. 15). Do not apply bank-loan terms to Kanungu loans; obtain signed member-loan agreements and worked examples.
- **Investment policy uses ranges, not just point targets.** The September board pack lists Kanungu 35–45%, quoted equities 25–45%, bonds 10–25%, cash 5–15%, Turbo private equity 10–20%, and real estate 0% at that time; it also lists minimum expected returns and other monitoring dimensions (board pack p. 16; May minutes p. 13). Confirm current policy, effective dates, denominator and treatment of overlapping ranges before calculating compliance. The domain schema can record dated bounds; confirm approved policy before using them for compliance decisions.
- **Bookings have a hold state and partial fulfilment.** The statements' booking tracker shows fulfilled, unfulfilled and borrower-held requests, total demand and issued amounts (statements p. 28; board pack p. 10). Confirm hold/resume history, ordering, and treatment of repeated bookings before automating fulfilment; the application records hold/resume history separately from the stored booking status; it does not infer queue position or allocate funds.
- **Dividend policy is time-dependent.** The 2020 prospectus states a 25% net-income policy (p. 8), while the 2026 minutes and board pack report a 40% policy decided at the 11th AGM through 2030 (minutes p. 28; board pack p. 22). Confirm the 2026 basis, eligibility, approvals and distribution rounding against AGM records before calculating or posting dividends.
- **Share trading can involve multiple buyers and partial withdrawals.** The trade register records dated, priced purchases and sales and a partial withdrawal (statements p. 36; board pack p. 22). The 2020 prospectus discusses separate company buyback and onward-sale prices and a 90-day lock-in (prospectus p. 17). These are historical, not confirmed current rules. Validate settlement and allocation with source records.
- **Financial statements require reconciliation and validation.** The August 2026 statements include income, financial position, equity, cash-flow and detailed account notes; they show revaluation reserves and payables, and explicitly report an unbalancing figure (statements pp. 8–12). The apparent auditor report refers to 2024 and includes an uncompleted date/signature area (pp. 6–7), while the call says no external audit had yet been completed. Do not infer that the 2026 statements were audited, that they balance, or that any particular accounting framework has been approved; obtain signed statements and policies, reconcile balances, and clarify the audit status.
- **Source registers are snapshots, not event histories.** The 2022 register contains 57 numbered rows and 70,281 total shares (2022 shareholding PDF p. 1); the 2026 statements include a later shareholding snapshot and report 90,000 shares for earnings per share (statements pp. 8, 37). Verify effective dates, joint holdings, membership changes, bonus issues, trades and totals before migration. PDF text extraction can misalign names and quantities; inspect the original and reconcile each row.
- **The reports also include external borrowings and member deposits.** Bank-loan principal, interest, maturity and settlement are shown separately (statements p. 34); member deposits include unpaid dividends and excess payments (p. 35). These are distinct from loans to members and should not be folded into the member-loan ledger without approved mappings.
